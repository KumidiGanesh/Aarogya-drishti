# Oral health educational illustrations

All 24 disease illustrations are original, non-graphic vector diagrams created for the AAROGYA DRISHTI demonstration platform. Each uses a simplified mouth illustration with a distinct visual motif based on the topic's general educational description. They are not photographs, do not show real patients, and must not be used to identify, diagnose, or rule out a disease. A qualified clinician must assess any health concern.

Two clinical reference photos are shown when their external sources are available. They are not diagnostic images, and the original diagrams are shown as a fallback if a photo cannot load. The images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons at runtime, so an unavailable network/source falls back to the local illustration:

- Oral leukoplakia: [National Cancer Institute photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leukoplakia.jpg), photographer unknown; public domain. The image is served from Wikimedia Commons.
- Oral submucous fibrosis: [Saivishnu.ponnapalli photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oral_submucous_fibrosis.jpg), licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The image is served from Wikimedia Commons.

The other 22 entries use original, non-graphic diagrams rendered as inline SVG by `client/src/components/diseases/DiseaseIllustration.jsx`. No photo is presented for a condition without a source image and reuse terms verified for this demo.

Medical translations and educational content have not been independently reviewed by a clinician or native-language medical translator. The site displays a review notice and is for demonstration use only.
